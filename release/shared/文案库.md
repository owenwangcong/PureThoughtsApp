### 繁体

```
善護念是共修團體的功課記錄工具:記錄每日功課,查看個人統計與共修總量。

【每日報數】
• 功課具體到經名:阿彌陀經、金剛經、地藏經、大悲咒、楞嚴咒、念佛、靜坐等,可自行新增功課項
• 一鍵重複上次報數;補報計入報數當天
• 可替其他同修代報,對方會收到通知並可自行刪除該筆記錄
• 記錄可修改、可刪除;個人明細僅本人可見

【統計】
• 今日、近 14 天趨勢、歷史累計,均以「我的」與「全體」並排呈現
• 全體一欄顯示共修總量與當日已報人數,不提供成員排行
• 連續用功天數僅本人可見

【發願】
• 為某項功課設定目標與期限(7 / 21 / 49 / 100 / 365 天或自訂)
• 進度依報數自動累計,達成時顯示回向文;到期未達成自動結束

【活動與佛曆】
• 活動日曆疊加佛曆:農曆日期、佛教紀念日、十齋日
• 支持每週循環活動,可單次改期或取消
• 活動提醒分提前一天、開始前 30 分鐘、開始時三檔,可按類別訂閱與設定免打擾時段
• 活動可附時間表與 PDF 資料

【聞法與學修】
• YouTube 直播開播通知,可在 App 內觀看;Webex 會議室應用內加入
• 在線經本;講法問答檢索(依主題與關鍵詞)
• 學修問答:向管理員提問,收到回覆時推送通知

【工具(離線可用)】

【介面】
• 繁簡雙語,可隨時切換
• 全域字號調整至兩倍,版面自適應
• 淺色 / 深色雙主題,可跟隨系統

【帳號與隱私】
• 註冊只需用戶名與密碼,電子郵箱選填(僅用於找回密碼)
• 個人修行明細僅本人可見

• 提供檢舉與封鎖功能,由管理員處理
```

### 英文(仅在开英文本地化时用)

```
PureThoughts is a practice log for Buddhist practice groups: record your daily practice and
view personal statistics alongside the group's totals.

DAILY LOG
• Log by the specific text — Amitabha Sutra, Diamond Sutra, Ksitigarbha Sutra, Great
  Compassion Mantra, Shurangama Mantra, Buddha-name recitation, sitting meditation. Custom
  items can be added.
• Repeat the previous entry with one tap; back-filled entries count on the day they are logged.
• Log on behalf of another member; they are notified and can remove the entry themselves.
• Entries can be edited or removed. Personal detail is visible only to the owner.

STATISTICS
• Today, a 14-day trend and lifetime totals, each shown as "mine" next to the group's.
• The group column shows totals and the number of members who logged today. No per-member
  ranking is provided.
• Consecutive-day count is visible only to the owner.

VOWS
• Set a target and period (7 / 21 / 49 / 100 / 365 days, or custom).
• Progress accrues from logged entries; a dedication verse is shown on completion, and
  unfinished vows close automatically at the end date.

EVENTS AND BUDDHIST CALENDAR
• Calendar overlaid with the Buddhist almanac: lunar dates, festivals, the ten fasting days.
• Weekly recurring events; single occurrences can be rescheduled or cancelled.
• Reminders at three offsets — one day before, 30 minutes before, at start — with per-category
  subscription and a do-not-disturb window.
• Events can carry an agenda and PDF attachments.

TEACHINGS AND Q&A
• YouTube livestream alerts with in-app viewing; Webex meeting rooms joined in-app.
• Online scripture library; searchable teaching Q&A by topic and keyword.
• Study Q&A: ask an administrator and get a push notification on reply.

TOOLS (work offline)
• Meditation timer: presets or any custom length from 1 minute to 12 hours; preparation,
  interval and closing bells; screen stays awake; the session can be logged in one tap.
• Mala counter: full-screen tap counting with a buzz on each full round (27 / 54 / 108 / 1080);
  the count is stored locally and can be logged in one tap.

INTERFACE
• Traditional and Simplified Chinese, switchable at any time.
• Global text size up to 200% with adaptive layout; light and dark themes.

ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY
• Registration requires only a username and password; email is optional (password recovery).
• Accounts can be deleted from inside the app; personal data is removed and past entries
  remain only as anonymised totals.
• Reporting and blocking are available, handled by administrators.
```
